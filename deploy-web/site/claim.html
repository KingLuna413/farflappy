How claiming will work

1. Play with your Pilot in the Arena. 2. Your replay is re-simulated and verified on the server. 3. Verified scores and wins unlock a share of that week's pool. 4. Claim sends $FLAP to the Pilot owner's wallet, with any delegation split applied.

$FLAP rewards are separate from mint fees. Mint fees are held by the FlapPilot contract until the owner withdraws them. Reward $FLAP comes from the Arena pool, planned at 40% of total supply.
